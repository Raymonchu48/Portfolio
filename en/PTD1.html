PROJECT DETAILS

AUTHORRAMON ALBERTO CURBALAN VEGA

PROGRAMMEGeneración Digital PYMES

PERIOD2024–2025

01

Assessment and strategic reflection

Internal analysis

Assessment of the company's current digital maturity, examining internal processes, technology use, digital culture and capacity to adapt to change.

(This section incorporates the results of the digital maturity self-assessment.)

External analysis

A study of the competitive environment, identifying innovative companies, digital business models, technology strategies and products offered by competitors and sector leaders.

SWOT analysis

  • Weaknesses: current internal limitations.
  • Threats: external environmental risks.
  • Strengths: key capabilities and resources.
  • Opportunities: digital growth options.
PRIORITY AREA

Initial definition of action areas

Identification of priority areas for action: customer experience, processes, infrastructure, products, people and the business model.

02

Transforming the customer experience

Customer profile - Buyer Persona

Definition of the ideal customer, considering personal and professional characteristics, habits, motivations, frustrations and goals.

Empathy map

An in-depth analysis of what the customer thinks, feels, sees, hears, says and does, to better understand their real needs and behaviour.

Business Model Canvas

A visual representation of the business model: value proposition, customer segments, channels, relationships, resources, activities, costs and revenue.

Customer journey map

Analysis of every stage in the customer lifecycle: discovery, consideration, purchase, service and after-sales, identifying touchpoints, emotions and opportunities for improvement.

03

Enabling digital transformation

Digital Transformation Canvas

Customer focus Technologies Cloud and data Digital business Process engineering Digital culture and leadership Digital marketing
TECHNOLOGY

Technologies to improve or implement

Identification of necessary technology tools, their current implementation level, strategic priority and estimated budget.

04

Managing change

Objectives and KPIs

Definition of short- and medium-term SMART objectives, together with key performance indicators to measure the plan's success.

Action plan

Development of initiatives, tasks, owners, deadlines and budgets, including a prioritisation matrix for quick wins.

Required investment

Estimation of short-, medium- and long-term investment, aligned with the plan's strategic objectives.

Change leadership team

Identification of the people responsible for driving digital transformation within the organisation.

05

Training and conclusions

Training plan

Definition of the training activities needed to equip the team with the required digital skills.

Conclusion

The Digital Transformation Plan establishes a clear, realistic roadmap to improve the company's competitiveness, efficiency and sustainability through the strategic use of technology.

DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION PLAN

A roadmap to modernise, measure and sustain change.
