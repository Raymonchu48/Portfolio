CASE STUDY // SIMULATED ORGANISATION

Redes S.A. · headquarters + two regional branches. The topology and controls are explanatory proposals; they do not represent an actual inventory or verified metrics.

01

Architecture and segmentation

The proposal connects a headquarters to two branches through dedicated links or VPN tunnels. Segmentation separates Administration, Technical Support, Development and Guests, restricting inter-VLAN routing to authorised functional needs.

02

Stack and security controls

Segmentation

VLANs, inter-VLAN routing and access rules/ACLs based on the principle of least privilege.

Perimeter

Firewall, IDS/IPS, authentication, encryption and remote VPN access.

Operations

Unified management, telemetry, logs, alerts and configuration backups.

Connectivity

Managed switches, QoS, appropriately sized cabling and IPsec VPN when the Internet provides transport.

03

Execution plan

01 · Analysis02 · Selection03 · Installation04 · Segmentation05 · Security06 · Monitoring07 · Validation08 · Documentation09 · Production

Each phase is linked to evidence: inventory and requirements, a selection matrix, a physical diagram, permitted communications, access policies, an operations dashboard, test records, a manual and commissioning.

04

Incident: duplicate IP address

The commissioning scenario involves a duplicate IP address at headquarters, causing intermittent connectivity loss. The procedure separates confirmation, tracing, containment, correction, verification and prevention.

DETECT→IDENTIFY→CORRECT→VALIDATE→PREVENT

ipconfig /all · arp -a · ping <IP_address> · ipconfig /release · ipconfig /renew

05

Monitoring, continuity and acceptance

The proposed operation monitors availability and latency, centralises syslog, uses SNMPv3 where supported, schedules configuration backups and reviews inventory, DHCP, permissions and patches. Acceptance tests cover inter-site communication, guest isolation, remote access, the absence of further IP collisions, alerts and backup restoration.

PROJECT VALUE

Network design across the full lifecycle.

The case demonstrates requirements gathering, physical and logical design, segmentation, defence in depth, centralised operations, validation and systematic incident management, presented as an academic study and architecture proposal.
